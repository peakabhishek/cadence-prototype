Compared with Cadence look B on [#54](https://github.com/peakabhishek/cadence/issues/54), the [#58](https://github.com/peakabhishek/cadence/issues/58) Review sheet, and Design: reviews on [#55](https://github.com/peakabhishek/cadence/pull/55) and [#60](https://github.com/peakabhishek/cadence/pull/60).
